## ❓ Quick FAQs

A: Yes! Edit `app/globals.css`

**Q: Can I add new commands?**
A: Yes! Add to `lib/terminalData.ts` and `InteractiveTerminal.tsx`

A: Push to GitHub, Vercel auto-deploys

A: Yes! Fully responsive on all devices

A: Give Vercel URL to employers/recruiters
